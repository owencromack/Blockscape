Game Menu

How to Play

• Use the directional pad to move.

• Drag the right side of the screen to look around.

• Tap a block to select it, then press BREAK.

• Aim at a block and press PLACE to add a block.

• Choose blocks using the bottom hotbar.

• Your saved world stays on this browser and device.
