🧱 BlockScape

Explore, build, craft, and survive in your own block world.

Move with the D-pad or WASD. Drag the right side to look.

Game Menu

How to Play

Move: WASD / arrow keys or D-pad.

Look: drag the right side of the screen.

Break / punch: E or BREAK. Place: Q or PLACE.

Jump: Space or JUMP. Eat: EAT.

Tap a hotbar slot to select it. Open Inventory to craft items.

Survival: mine resources, eat food, and avoid slimes at night.

Creative: unlimited blocks and flight (tap JUMP while airborne).
